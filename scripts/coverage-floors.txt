# The lowest statement coverage, in percent, that each package may have.
# scripts/coverage-floors checks it against `go test -cover ./...`. Raise a
# floor when you raise the coverage; do not lower one to get a change through.
#
# Floors sit a few points under the measured value, so a small change does not
# trip them but a package that loses its tests does. compile, reconcile, kobj
# and signer were measured near 100 after their unit tests were written; the
# floor of 90 keeps them there. A package with no statements needs no line.
#
#   package             floor
applier                 80
bundle                  80
cmd/rolekeeper          5
compile                 90
conformance             88
kobj                    90
kubeapi                 78
reconcile               90
signer                  90
signer/jws              85
signer/keys             75
signer/revlog           78
signer/roottrust        72
